# Resume Ranking Study — instructions for rankers

## What this is
A friend is testing whether an AI resume-scoring tool agrees with human
judgment. You get 10 short resumes (in `resumes/`, labeled R01–R10).
All names and details are fake.

## What to do (takes ~15 minutes)
1. Read all 10 resumes in `resumes/`.
2. Rank them from **best to worst** as if you were hiring for a junior
   software engineering role. Consider: clarity, relevant skills,
   quantified achievements, overall professionalism.
3. Fill in the ranking below and send it back. Ties are allowed but try
   to avoid them — a strict 1–10 ordering is most useful.

Optional: one line on what separated your top 3 from the rest.

## Rules
- Do NOT discuss with other rankers before submitting.
- Do NOT try to guess which resume the AI scored highest — rank by your
  own judgment only.
- Rankers: aim for 3–5 people total.
